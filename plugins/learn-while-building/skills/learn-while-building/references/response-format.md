# Response format

Use two layers for meaningful updates.

## Layer 1: 30-second summary

Place this first. Use the heading `30-second summary` when a heading helps.

Answer five points in compact prose or a short list:

1. What happened?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What changed?
4. What is the useful lesson?
5. What was verified, and what remains open?

The summary must stand on its own. Target 70 to 130 words. Shorter is fine when the work is simple.

## Layer 2: useful detail

Only include detail that helps the user understand, verify, or continue the work. Prefer this order:

1. The mental model
2. The project example
3. The evidence or test
4. One small check for understanding, when useful
5. The next step, when one remains

Do not repeat the summary sentence by sentence. Do not bury failures, risks, uncertainty, or unverified behavior in this layer.

## Trivial messages

Do not force the full format onto greetings, one-line clarifications, or tiny factual answers. Keep those natural.

## Language rules

- Use plain language and short paragraphs.
- Define unfamiliar terms in place.
- Use no emojis.
- Use no em dashes.
- Avoid hype and generic praise.
- Avoid phrases such as `unlock`, `delve`, `game-changing`, `seamlessly`, and `revolutionary`.
- Do not claim mastery, professional status, or verified understanding without evidence.
